# DELIVERABLE_ASSEMBLED.md template (v11)

Written by the closer (sections 1 to 13, once) and assembled by Dispatch with the verifier file (section 14). Read in this order at breakfast: 14, then 7, then 10, then 1.

```
1 THE RESULT
<the usable answer, plan, spec, code, or document. For build, solve, strategy: the artifact itself.>

2 WHAT SURVIVED
<surviving option(s) with provenance tags. If two survive, say two survive.>

3 WHY IT SURVIVED
<every PASSED claim, its check result, its provenance tag>

4 OBJECTIVE RESULTS
<EXPERIMENT or HYBRID: baseline (value, noise, environment), each experiment id, hypothesis, delta, guardrails, KEEP / REVERT / INCONCLUSIVE. Else: not applicable>

5 WHAT DIED AND WHY
<every option killed across all stages and the FAILED claim or hard constraint that killed it (every kill is EARNED); then DEPRIORITIZED options, still standing, with the reasons>

6 TRADE-OFFS
<conflicts between surviving options or between objectives, stated, not resolved by arithmetic>

7 OUTSIDE REVIEW
<every HIT, GAP, HOLD verbatim, its check result (PASSED / FAILED / JUDGEMENT CALL / NOT TESTABLE / BLOCKED / INCONCLUSIVE / HOLD-ACCEPTED / HOLD-REJECTED), what the closer did with it. Or: NO OUTSIDE REVIEW, and why>

8 STILL OPEN
<judgement calls, NOT TESTABLE, BLOCKED, INCONCLUSIVE items, each with what would settle it>

9 CONFIDENCE
<High / Medium / Low per surviving option, with the reason>

10 RUN INTEGRITY
<earned kills vs deprioritized counts; stages run in order; STOP_REASON; seats failed or retired; closer swaps; review ran yes/no; flags: DEPRIORITIZED_GT_EARNED, EMPTY_OPEN_LIST, ALL_CLEAN, REDUCED_CREW, NO_OUTSIDE_REVIEW, NO_VERIFIER, NO_EXECUTOR, REVIEW_UNCHECKABLE, CLOSER_SWAPPED, PROVISIONAL, LEAK_SUSPECTED, GATE_DEFAULTED, CONTAMINATION, CAPTURE_PARTIAL, PAYLOAD_BLOCKED>

11 EFFICIENCY
<model calls, elapsed time, experiments run, tokens where reported>

12 NEXT QUESTION
<the ONE highest-value next question or experiment>

13 VERIFY BEFORE RELYING   (only if the ask touches law, tax, patents, medicine, or compliance)
<each claim a professional should confirm, the question to ask, the source it rests on. This is analysis, not professional advice.>

14 PRIVATE DOCUMENT VERIFICATION   (appended by Dispatch from final/verifier.md, never merged back)
CONTRADICTIONS / CONFIRMED / NOT COVERED / MATERIAL OMISSIONS
<a CONTRADICTION marks the run PROVISIONAL>
```
