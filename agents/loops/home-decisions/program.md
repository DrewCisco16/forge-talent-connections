# PROGRAM — HOME decision journal

**Lane:** `HOME` · **Type:** decision journal (deliberately not an optimization
loop) · **Node:** MacBook Air 15 M4 · **Cadence:** weekly
**Prepend:** `../_LOOP-PREAMBLE.md`

---

## Why there is no optimization loop here, stated plainly

An optimization loop over personal, family and investment decisions fails the
Measurability Gate on five of six counts (`../../11-autoresearch-loops.md` §5.4).
The one that matters most is **G4**:

> A loop optimizing backtested return will find **leverage, concentration, and
> survivorship bias.** Those are the three cheapest paths to an excellent
> backtest and a destroyed balance sheet.

Run one and you build a machine that generates confident, quantitative,
**fabricated** justifications for irreversible financial decisions. That is
against the truth standard, against downside protection, and against the
stewardship frame the whole enterprise rests on.

**So this is a journal, not an optimizer. It is the more valuable artifact.**

---

## Weekly procedure — for each open decision

```
1. CLAIM        Restate the decision as a falsifiable claim with a date.
                "X will be true by <date>" — not "X seems good".

2. BASE RATE    What normally happens in situations like this?
                If you do not know the base rate, SAY SO AND STOP.
                An unknown base rate is the finding.

3. PRE-MORTEM   It is three years from now and this failed badly.
                Write the story of how. Be specific and unkind.

4. INVERSION    What would have to be true for the OPPOSITE choice to be right?
                If you cannot argue the other side, you do not understand this one.

5. DISCONFIRMER Name the ONE fact that would change your mind,
                and exactly where that fact would come from.

6. RECORD       Decision · reasoning · expected outcome · review date.

7. REVIEW       On review dates: compare what happened to what you WROTE.
                Score the REASONING. Never the outcome.
                A good decision can have a bad outcome, and the reverse is
                more dangerous, because it teaches the wrong lesson.
```

## The only honest metric here

**Calibration** — how often the recorded reasoning turns out to have been right
*for the reason written down*. It accumulates over years. There is no weekly
number, and any weekly number would be noise dressed as signal.

## Absolute limits

❌ **Never recommends a specific security, allocation, or transaction**
❌ **Never produces a return estimate, probability, or expected value** — no
   dataset, no calculation, no number (`_SHARED-PREAMBLE` §3)
❌ **Never touches a brokerage, bank, payment, or payroll surface.** Money
   movement is a one-way door (`07` §6)
❌ **Browser control is OFF for this lane** (`12` §3). No exceptions — the
   downside of an agent in a profile authenticated to family finances is not
   bounded by anything
❌ **Never reads a lane it was not given.** It reads what Andrew hands it
❌ **Never optimizes a family relationship.** Stewardship is not a delegable
   function and a spouse is not a metric

## What it is genuinely for

Making your reasoning **retrievable and checkable later**. In three years the
question will not be "what did I decide" — it will be "what did I believe, and
was I right for the reason I thought." Almost nobody can answer that. This is how
you can.
