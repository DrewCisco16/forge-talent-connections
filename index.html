The standard

Nothing here replaces two humans. A claim becomes trusted only after verification, and no vouch counts until two people have put their own names behind it, each tied to work they actually observed. No credential, institution, algorithm, seal, or recommendation substitutes for either human choice.

What is in the product

What counts here is a completed, accepted collaboration and the proof it leaves behind. Job placement is outside the product promise. FORGE Talent Connections complements LinkedIn and Handshake by centering on doing projects together; it replaces neither.

The Two-Human Vouch

No claim is trusted on one person's word. A vouch is a scoped statement by an accountable observer, tied to a named contribution, and it counts only when two humans have given theirs separately.

Project Spaces

Bounded projects with named reviewers, clear acceptance criteria, and milestones that seal when the work passes its checks.

Explainable AI Matching

Every factor shown, for and against. Suggested, not decided: a person reviews every match before anything is final, and an AI confidence level is never permission.

Talent Stories

Short verified video introductions, one swipe at a time, in place of a paper summary of yourself. Preview in the demo today; the full feature ships within the year.

Proof Builder

Assembles your Verified Portfolio from verified records only. No invented roles, dates, or numbers, ever.

Sealed Records and Seal Check

Exportable records that anyone can verify were not altered, and a public check that says so plainly.

AI Assistant

AI assists the work; human accountability stays visible. Built on Google Cloud Vertex AI, it can explain, draft, and research, it never writes to your record, and it can never vouch for a person.

Talent Signature

Your strengths, drawn only from your verified record, each with its evidence named. Never a score, a ranking, or a guess about who you are. Preview in the demo today.

Built for Veterans

Veteran verification, service sealed and honored, and roads built for what comes next after service.

Nothing opens without proof

Access is earned step by step, and each step needs its own evidence. If required identity, permission, integrity, or evidence is missing, the protected action does not proceed: it is denied or held for human review, never silently allowed.

  1. Verified organization
  2. Authorized invitation
  3. Verified collaborator
  4. Permitted project
  5. Reviewed contribution
  6. Scoped vouch
  7. Permitted export
