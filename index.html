The Standard

The Access Model

Nothing Opens Without Proof

The intended access model requires evidence at each step. These are design requirements, not a claim that live verification is available here. When identity, permission, integrity, or evidence cannot be proven, the protected action does not proceed. The system denies, holds, or escalates to a human reviewer. It never proceeds silently.

  1. Verified Organization The institution itself is identified first.
  2. Authorized Invitation Named sponsor, named recipient, single use.
  3. Verified Collaborator Identity, consent, and eligibility checked.
  4. Permitted Project Membership, approval, and least privilege.
  5. Reviewed Contribution Accepted against criteria by a named reviewer.
  6. Scoped Vouch An accountable statement about observed work.
  7. Permitted Export A sealed evidence package, released only with authority.

The Answers

Questions, Answered

Is FORGE Talent Connections a Job Board?

No. It is an invitation-only project collaboration network. What counts here is a completed, accepted collaboration and the proof it leaves behind. Job placement is outside the product promise.

How Do I Join?

By invitation. An invitation opens the door, but it is not a vouch. Joining takes two accountable human vouches, each earned separately through real work someone will put their name behind.

What Does the AI Do, and What Can It Never Do?

The planned AI features suggest matches with every factor shown, and explain, draft, and research. A person reviews every match before anything is final. It never writes to your record, and it can never vouch for a person.

What Happens When Something Cannot Be Verified?

The design requires the protected action to stop. It must deny, hold, or escalate to a human reviewer and state the outcome. Live enforcement is not available through this page.

Does the Preview Use Real Data?

No. The preview shows clearly labelled sample content while the backend is completed, and every sample screen is marked as a preview. Nothing in it pretends to be live verification.
