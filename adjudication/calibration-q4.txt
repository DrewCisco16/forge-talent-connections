FORGE TALENT CONNECTIONS — Q4 PLACEMENT REVIEW (DRAFT)

Placements closed: 14 in October, 9 in November, 12 in December.
That is 35 placements for the quarter.

Average placement fee was $18,500. At 35 placements the quarter billed
$665,000 in placement revenue.

Recruiting cost: 4 recruiters at $6,200 per month, across the three
months of the quarter, comes to $72,400.

Gross margin for the quarter is therefore $592,600.

Of the 35 placements, 40% converted from contract to permanent. That is
14 conversions.

Each conversion carries a $3,000 uplift, so conversion uplift added
$42,000.

Total quarter contribution, margin plus uplift, is $634,600.

Our stated target was $600,000. We beat the target by $34,600.

Cost per placement was $2,068.57.

If Q1 holds the same average fee and we add 3 recruiters, taking us to
7, straight-line capacity suggests 61 placements.
